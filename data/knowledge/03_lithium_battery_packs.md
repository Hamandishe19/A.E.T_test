# Category 03 — Lithium Battery Packs (LiFePO4)

Source: Anern "SOLAR CATALOG". Page references are **PDF page numbers**, with printed page numbers in brackets.

## Data notes for this category
- The `Cycle life` row for LPB-N and LPB-Nplus is printed as two values for four or five models. The mapping is not clear from the layout, so check the datasheet before quoting a cycle-life figure for a specific model.
- Battery packs in this category are in the rack / module style. The source does not give weights for the LPB-N, LPB-Nplus and LPB-R families.
