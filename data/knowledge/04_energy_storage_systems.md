# Category 04 — Energy Storage Systems (ESS)

Source: Anern "SOLAR CATALOG". Page references are **PDF page numbers**, with printed page numbers in brackets.

This category contains 5 product families and 10 modules or configurations. It covers integrated home and small-business storage units, the AN-MPSG-D inverter-plus-battery system, and containerised and cabinet C&I systems. Application solutions and project cases are in `06_system_overviews_components_and_projects.md`.

## 3. On & Off-Grid Cabinet Energy Storage System
- **Source:** PDF p.38 [printed 69–70]
- **Type:** High-capacity cabinet energy storage system for commercial and industrial (C&I) use. It combines power conversion, battery, power distribution, DC module, temperature control, fire protection and monitoring. It supports on-grid and off-grid operation.
- **Capacity range (as printed):** 105 kW – 241 kWh. ⚠️ The source prints two capacity ranges on this spread. This entry takes the first, and the containerised system takes the second (125 kW – 261 kWh).

**Applications (as printed):** peak shaving, frequency regulation, EV charging, solar plus storage, micro-grid, and self-consumption optimisation.

**Features (as printed):** all-in-one, turn-key and modular design; integrated battery, PCS, power distribution and DC module; temperature control; fire protection; flooding door magnet; monitoring and communication; comprehensive control of system status and risk; various working modes can be set; seamless transfer between on- and off-grid; strong load adaptability; perfect protection for inverters and batteries; modular PV controllers for easy expansion of PV capacity; prediction-based intelligent battery management.

**Power modules (as printed):** AC/DC power module 50 / 63 / 80 kW; AC/DC power module 105 kW; AC/DC power module 125 kW; DC/DC power module 50 / 63 kW; STS (static transfer switch) 200 / 500 kW.
