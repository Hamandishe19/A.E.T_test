# Category 02 — Solar Charge Controllers

Source: Anern "SOLAR CATALOG". Page references are **PDF page numbers**, with printed page numbers in brackets.

This category contains 2 product families (MPPT and PWM) and 13 modules.

---

## 1. MPPT Solar Controller — AN-MSC Series
- **Source:** PDF p.25–26 [printed 43–46] (one product family, spread over two pages)
- **Type:** Multi-level maximum power point tracking (MPPT) solar charge controller. The catalog states that MPPT increases the solar output power by 5–30% compared with PWM.
- **Modules:** `AN-MSC-20A`, `AN-MSC-30A`, `AN-MSC-40A`, `AN-MSC-60A` (p.25); `AN-MSC-80A`, `AN-MSC-100A`, `AN-MSC-140A`, `AN-MSC-80A` (96 V variant) (p.26)

| Module | Max charge current | DC system voltage (auto) | Max PV input voltage | Dimensions (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AN-MSC-20A | 20 A | 12 / 24 V | 60 V | 178 × 123.5 × 48 |
| AN-MSC-30A | 30 A | 12 / 24 V | 75 V | 195 × 133 × 55 |
| AN-MSC-40A | 40 A | 12 / 24 V | 100 V | 218 × 149 × 67 |
| AN-MSC-60A | 60 A | 12 / 24 / 48 V | 150 V | 265 × 191 × 95 |
| AN-MSC-80A | 80 A | 12 / 24 / 48 V | 150 V | 352 × 230 × 112 |
| AN-MSC-100A | 100 A | 12 / 24 / 48 V | 150 V | 352 × 230 × 112 |
| AN-MSC-140A | 140 A | 24 / 48 V | 450 V | 380 × 249 × 115 |
| AN-MSC-80A (96 V) | 80 A | 96 V | 450 V | 415 × 300 × 115 |

⚠️ The source lists "AN-MSC-80A" twice. The second column has a 96 V system voltage and a 450 V PV input, so it is treated here as a 96 V variant.

**Common parameters:** battery type selectable; LCD display; adjustable parameters; multiple protections; optional Bluetooth; Class V-0 flame-retardant enclosure. The source notes that the controller should be operated within its allowed ambient temperature range, and derated if that range is exceeded.

**Features (as printed):** multi-level MPPT technology; compared with PWM, MPPT improves control accuracy and increases solar panel output power by 5–30%.

## 2. PWM Solar Controller — AN-PSC Series
- **Source:** PDF p.27 [printed 47–48]
- **Type:** Pulse-width modulation (PWM) solar charge controller. It controls the solar panel charging the battery and regulates the charge and discharge conditions. It also controls the power from the solar cell and the battery to the load, according to load demand.
- **Modules:** `AN-PSC-10A`, `AN-PSC-20A`, `AN-PSC-30A`, `AN-PSC-40A`, `AN-PSC-60A`

| Module | Max charge current | DC system voltage (auto) | Max PV input voltage | Dimensions (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AN-PSC-10A | 10 A | 12 / 24 V | 50 V | 134 × 94 × 36 |
| AN-PSC-20A | 20 A | 12 / 24 V | 50 V | 134 × 94 × 36 |
| AN-PSC-30A | 30 A | 12 / 24 V | 50 V | 154 × 114 × 42 |
| AN-PSC-40A | 40 A | 12 / 24 V | 50 V | 154 × 114 × 42 |
| AN-PSC-60A | 60 A | 12 / 24 V | 50 V | 154 × 144 × 42 |

**Common parameters:** battery type selectable; LCD display; adjustable parameters; multiple protections; sensing function; Class V-0 flame-retardant enclosure. The source also notes derating for ambient temperature above the controller's allowed range.

**Features (as printed):** PWM charge control; regulates battery charge and discharge; controls power flow from solar and battery to load; sensing function; LCD display; protections.

## Data notes for this category
